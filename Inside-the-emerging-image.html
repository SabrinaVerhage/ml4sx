A useful simplification.

This guide follows a classic text-conditioned latent diffusion pipeline. Other systems use diffusion transformers, flow matching, or autoregressive image tokens. There is no single architecture shared by every image model.

All images here are procedural illustrations drawn in your browser. The noise sliders blend a known illustration with seeded noise; they do not run learned denoising. The colored latent grids, vectors, attention maps, parameter changes and loss values are authored teaching diagrams, not measured model internals. A real generator does not know its finished image in advance.

Training learns parameters from examples. Generation uses those parameters and normally does not retrieve training-image fragments. This distinction does not rule out memorization, close reproduction, dataset bias or questions about consent and copyright.

Words are shown as readable units; real tokenizers may split them into smaller pieces. Image–text associations are learned statistically and are imperfect. One word does not own one image region.
