The map is not the machine

Many layers, many choices.

Companies rarely publish exactly how their filters work, and every product combines layers differently. This guide shows the common kinds of layers described in research papers and model documentation, using made-up examples and scores.

Nothing here runs a real classifier. The word-list check in step 5 really does check your text against the list shown, and nothing else. Every other score, position and percentage is authored to explain the idea.

Filtering decisions are value judgements. Rules written to block “explicit sexual content” often also block sex education, health information, queer art, sex workers’ safety information and sex-tech businesses. Who sets the threshold, and for whom, is a real question.
