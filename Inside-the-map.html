Real data, one point of view

Where this map comes from.

The 864 products come from one online shop’s vibrator catalogue (EasyToys), collected in January 2026. Each product photo was turned into grey tones and passed through CLIP (OpenAI’s ViT-B/32 model). Its 512 numbers were mixed with CLIP’s numbers for a short product description (70% image, 30% text, colour words removed), so products group by shape and function more than by colour.

The map uses UMAP with cosine distance (12 neighbours, minimum distance 0.3). The flat “PCA” view in step 4 uses principal component analysis. The neighbour lists use all 512 numbers, not the map. Product types come from the shop’s own categories.

Product photos belong to the shop, so they aren’t included in this repository. If a map-photos folder with the photos sits next to this file, the guide shows them; otherwise it shows labelled tiles.
